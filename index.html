THE VILLA

Slow down.
Stay close to nature.

Welcome to Lake Villa & Farm House, a private one-bedroom stay in Acharigama, Thalawa, Anuradhapura.

The villa accommodates up to two guests and offers a peaceful setting surrounded by greenery, farmland and the lake.

Breakfast is provided, with air conditioning and private parking for your comfort.

A handcrafted outdoor space surrounded by natural timber, branches and greenery — a distinctive part of the property.

Nature at your doorstep.

There is no swimming pool. Instead, the property sits within a landscape shaped by the lake, fields and surrounding greenery.

Lake access and swimming conditions should be confirmed with the host before entering the water.
